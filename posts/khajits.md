This is not a serious city guide. This is a bonus quest for the true rulers of the warm streets: the Moroccan cats, also known here as the local Khajiit guild.

They were everywhere. Sleeping in patches of sun, guarding doorways, inspecting restaurant tables, watching the souks with ancient merchant energy, and generally behaving like every alley was part of their personal province. If a Khajiit has wares when you have coin, these ones mostly had naps, attitude, and extremely strong opinions about personal space.

![Orange and white cat hiding behind a palm trunk on white gravel](khajits2.jpg)

The first recruit appeared in classic stealth mode, half hidden behind a palm trunk, staring like I had entered the wrong part of the map without enough sneak skill. Very small. Very serious. Clearly in charge.

## Khajiit Has Naps

The Elder Scrolls part is not subtle, I know. But Morocco kept giving exactly that Khajiit feeling: warm stone, bright markets, shadowy corners, soft paws, suspicious eyes, and the sense that someone might offer you moon sugar, spices, or a slightly questionable travel deal if you stood still for too long.

![Orange and white cat lounging on a chair and looking unimpressed](khajits3.jpg)

This one had already claimed the chair, the shade, and probably the entire inn. There was no negotiation screen. The quest marker simply moved around the cat.

![Small black and white kitten sitting on a brick ledge in the sun](khajits4.jpg)

The tiny ones were the most dangerous, obviously. This kitten looked cute, but also like it would send you to collect three impossible herbs from a cave if you made eye contact for too long.

![Fluffy grey cat sitting on sunlit stone pavement](khajits5.jpg)

Some cats looked like travelling merchants. Some looked like minor nobles. This one looked like it had retired from adventuring after one too many dungeon crawls and now simply judged the public from a comfortable bit of pavement.

![Ginger cat beside colorful tagine dishes in the souk](khajits6.jpg)

And then came the actual market Khajiit. No wares visible on the cat itself, but the tagines beside it made the whole scene feel like a shop menu: ceramics, sunshine, and one orange shopkeeper who might accept coin or fish.

![Two orange and white cats standing in garden shade](khajits7.jpg)

The garden faction had a softer mood. Less bargaining, more quiet surveillance from behind leaves. They looked like siblings, apprentices, or two NPCs waiting for the player to stop taking photos and continue the quest.

![Black and white kitten looking up from a brick garden edge](khajits8.jpg)

This little one had full "new companion unlocked" energy. Too small for combat, possibly excellent at morale, definitely going to walk directly in front of every door.

![Grey tabby cat looking up beside a warm brick wall](khajits9.jpg)

Some of them had faces that made the Elder Scrolls joke feel too easy. Narrow eyes, warm walls, dusty ground, and that slightly offended Khajiit expression, as if I had asked the wrong dialogue option.

## The Souk Guild

The souks already feel like an inventory screen that has escaped into real life: rugs, scarves, ceramics, lamps, spices, metalwork, leather, colors everywhere. Adding cats to that made everything better. Suddenly every stall had a guild master.

![Black and white cat sleeping in front of a rug shop](khajits11.jpg)

This one was not working hard, but leadership is about delegation. The rugs were displayed, the colors were glowing, the doorway was guarded, and the cat was technically on duty.

![Ginger cat yawning dramatically in the sun](khajits12.jpg)

The orange cats especially understood performance. This yawn had theatre. This was not tiredness; this was a full spell animation.

![Tabby cat standing in tall green grass](khajits13.jpg)

Then there were the stealth archers. One moment visible in the grass, the next almost gone. Morocco gave them perfect cover: tall plants, hard light, and enough shadow for a whole sneaking tutorial.

![Two tabby cats sitting by a port sign in Essaouira](khajits14.jpg)

The port cats had a different rank. Less soft guild, more city guard. They sat by the curb like they were checking documents, watching the road, and deciding who was allowed through the gate.

## Innkeepers And Chair Owners

Restaurant cats are their own faction. They do not visit the restaurant. They own it. Humans are tolerated because we bring chairs, plates, crumbs, and occasionally the illusion of management.

![Calico cat meowing indoors on a woven floor](khajits15.jpg)

This one had something to say and no patience for our limited language skills. It felt less like a meow and more like a quest update I was failing to understand.

![White cat sleeping on the seat of a parked motorbike](khajits16.jpg)

The motorbike sleeper was perfect. No bed, no problem. If the seat is warm and slightly inconvenient for everyone else, then it is legally a cat bed.

![Small tabby kitten curled up on a red patterned rug](khajits17.jpg)

This kitten was the soft side of the whole questline. Not a merchant, not a guard, not a noble. Just a tiny exhausted traveler on a rug, recharging before the next adventure.

![Orange and white cat sitting neatly on a doormat](khajits18.jpg)

Some cats understood doorways as sacred territory. This one had the exact expression of an innkeeper waiting to hear whether I had a reservation.

![Three cats gathered around a sunny garden ledge](khajits19.jpg)

The garden council had assembled here: one stretching, one observing, one half asleep in the greenery. A very convincing argument for never rushing through the optional areas.

![White cat sitting between cafe chairs on stone pavement](khajits20.jpg)

This cat had the cleanest "you may approach" posture of the group. It sat between cafe chairs like a tiny bouncer with no clipboard and complete authority.

![Tabby cat curled on a bright blue bench](khajits21.jpg)

The blue bench was a good throne. Weathered paint, dappled shade, a tabby curled into the perfect royal shape. Somewhere in Tamriel, a Khajiit caravan would understand.

![Tortoiseshell cat resting in a doorway patch of sun](khajits22.jpg)

The doorway cats were my favorite because they made ordinary thresholds feel important. You did not just enter a shop or a cafe. You passed a small, sleepy guardian with excellent camouflage.

![Tabby cat stretched across a restaurant chair beside a set table](khajits23.jpg)

This is the restaurant owner. I do not mean mascot. I mean owner. The table was set for humans, but the chair situation had already been decided by local law.

![Two cats sleeping beside tall garden grass](khajits24.jpg)

Every good quest needs a rest area. This one came with ornamental grass, warm stone, two sleeping cats, and the feeling that the main quest could wait another five minutes.

![Orange and white kitten licking its nose on a cafe chair](khajits25.jpg)

This little one looked innocent, but that is how they get you. One second you are taking a photo, the next you are emotionally invested in a side character with no dialogue and huge eyes.

![Grey and white cat rubbing against a chair leg on patterned tiles](khajits10.jpg)

By the end, the pattern was obvious: every warm tile, every chair leg, every doorway, every bench, every patch of shade had already been claimed. We were just passing through their province.

## Side Quest Complete

So this is their post. Not the palaces, not the gardens, not the souks, not the famous viewpoints, but the small furry citizens between all of that. The Khajiit of Morocco had no need for coin, no interest in our schedule, and absolutely no respect for the main quest.

Honestly, fair.
