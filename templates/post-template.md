Open with the strongest feeling from the place. Keep it specific: the light, the first street, the sound, the weather, the view, or one small detail that made the day feel memorable.

Add a second paragraph that gives context. This can explain why the place matters, how you got there, what kind of trip it was, or what made it worth writing about.

Write a short paragraph after every image. Use it to connect the photo to the story instead of only describing what is visible.

## First Impression

Use sections to give the post a clear rhythm. Good section angles: first impression, history, architecture, food, landscape, favorite details, practical notes, or final thoughts.

![Descriptive image alt text](image-3.jpg)

Keep image alt text human and concrete. It should make sense for accessibility and also read nicely as a caption in the post.

## What Stayed With Me

This section is a good place for the more personal part of the note. What did the place make you notice? What would you remember later? What surprised you?

![Descriptive image alt text](image-4.jpg)

Between photos, add enough text to make the post feel intentional. One to three paragraphs is usually enough.

## Final Notes

End with a clear closing feeling. It can be practical, reflective, or personal, but it should leave the post feeling finished rather than simply stopping after the last image.
